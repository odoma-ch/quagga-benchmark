Chat with Quagga

Want to explore a knowledge graph without writing a single query? The Quagga agent lets you ask questions in plain language and handles the SPARQL for you: it translates your question into a query, runs it, and brings back an answer.

Transparency is built in. Rather than hiding the technical machinery, the agent shows you the SPARQL query behind every answer, so you can inspect it, verify it, and learn from it. The goal isn't to replace SPARQL, but to lower the barrier to entry while helping you understand how knowledge graphs are queried.

Note: the Quagga agent is in early development (alpha) and we're actively working to expand its capabilities. You may encounter rough edges, and for now the agent can only query one knowledge graph at a time. Not all knowledge graphs in the Quagga benchmark are available in the agent yet, but we're gradually enabling new ones.
